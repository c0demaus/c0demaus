💖 Sparkle Your Headlights: Easy Sanding Guide 💖

Step 1: Get Ready

  • ✨Clean your headlights
  • 🎀Tape the edges to protect your car
  • 💅Grab 1000 & 2000 grit sanding discs

Step 2: Start Sanding (Wet is Best!)

  • 💧Wet the sanding discs & headlights with water
  • 🔄Gently sand using the 1000 grit disc in small circles
  • 🧊Keep everything wet and cool to protect the plastic

Step 3: Smooth It Out

  • 🎀Switch to 2000 grit disc for extra smoothness
  • 💦Sand softly, keeping water handy
  • 💫Sand evenly for a flawless finish

Step 4: Polish Time

  • 🎉Buff your headlights with a microfiber cloth
  • 🌟Shine them up until they sparkle like new

Step 5: Seal & Protect

  • ☀️🛡️Apply a UV sealant to keep that clear sparkle safe from sun damage
💖 Pro Tips:
- Go slow and steady – no rush! ⏳
- Keep it wet to avoid any damage 💦
- Be gentle, your headlights deserve the best care 💖
